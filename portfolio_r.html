About

A little about the mind behind the code.

I'm studying Information Science and Engineering at Brindavan College of Engineering in Bengaluru, working toward my Bachelor of Engineering since 2023 with a CGPA of 8/10.

Full-stack web development is the thread through my work. I build with React.js, Node.js, Express.js and MongoDB, and I'm comfortable in SQL and database management. My projects so far include an AI-assisted recruitment platform, an algorithm visualizer and a real-time collaborative coding workspace.

I'm especially curious about AI and Generative AI, from prompt engineering to AI/ML-driven applications, and I'm now diving deeper into machine learning. I also like seeing data come to life in Tableau and Power BI.

I approach problems analytically, notice the details, and learn new tools quickly. I enjoy working with a team and I'm always looking for the next technology to explore.
